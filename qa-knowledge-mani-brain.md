# QA KNOWLEDGE — MANI BRAIN LAYER (V20)

This file upgrades the SUBSTANCE of QA feedback. It changes NOTHING about format,
message structure, section order, markers, or posting flow — those are locked by the
main prompt. Use this layer to make every piece of feedback specific, evidence-based,
and grounded in what actually wins deals at RoofIgnite, instead of generic sales theory.

THE 15 EVIDENCE-BASED COACHING RULES (cite these in feedback when relevant)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. PRICE GATE. No setup-fee or retainer dollar figure before minute 25 unless the
   prospect explicitly demanded pricing 2+ times. (Wins pitch at 25.8-41.0 min;
   losses at ~23.4.)
2. VALUE STACK BEFORE PRICE. All core mechanism components (creative/messaging,
   funnel, speed-to-lead, pixel) mentioned before the first price. (~85-95% of wins.)
3. DATED NEXT STEP OR PAYMENT — EVERY CALL. Final 10 minutes must contain a processed
   payment/invoice or an explicit day + clock time confirmed by the prospect.
   "I'll get back to you" without a time = fail. (Bronson wins 93%; his losses fail 84%.)
4. NO UNPROMPTED DISCOUNTS. Any fee/retainer reduction must be preceded by an explicit
   prospect price objection AND paired with a named trade (testimonial, referral, start
   date). A discount within 60s of a stall with no ask = fail. (0 conversions on
   unprompted discounts across all lost cohorts.)
5. RETAINER FLOOR. Retainer never quoted below $4,000 + 10% of ad spend without a
   restructure (rev-share / % of revenue); any bare retainer cut = fail.
6. DECISION-MAKER CHECK BY MINUTE 15. A question establishing who else is involved in
   the decision, inside the first 15 minutes. (21-25% of Mani's own losses failed this.)
7. FINANCIAL QUALIFICATION BEFORE PRICE. A cash/credit/resources question before the
   first price mention. (Both refunded deals — 119044625, 130254864 — skipped it.)
8. PERSONAL-PAIN PROBE ATTEMPTED. At least one "why does that matter to you / what does
   that change for you or your family" question per first-touch discovery.
   (52% on Bronson wins vs 11% on Mani losses.)
9. PAIN RE-INVOKED AT CLOSE. Within 5 minutes before the ask, the closer references a
   pain or goal the prospect stated earlier.
10. OBJECTION ATTEMPTS: >=2, <=3. Each blocking objection gets a second different-angle
    attempt or restructure; a single pass then call-end = fail; a 4th press = fail.
    (63% of Bronson losses were one-pass.)
11. BOUNDARY STOP. After a stated same-day-decision refusal ("not deciding today",
    "sleep on it"), zero payment asks for the rest of the call; only a dated follow-up
    close is permitted. (114388751, 113059415, 111932543 all violated and lost.)
12. BELIEF COMMITMENT BEFORE ASK. A "do you believe this would work for you?"
    confirmation with an affirmative response must precede the first payment ask.
13. NO VERBAL INCOME OR REFUND PROMISES. Flag any unwritten revenue guarantee or ad-hoc
    refund promise beyond written 28-day terms.
14. TALK-RATIO BAND 45-70%. Above 75% flags lecture mode. (Winning averages 54-64%.)
15. HARD COMPLIANCE FLAGS. Wrong prospect name 2+ times, any ethnicity/race remark,
    another client's data visible on screen — instant human review.

WHAT WINS AND LOSES DEALS HERE (the evidence behind your feedback)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- LOSSES DIE AT THE CLOSE, not at rapport. Lost-call close-strength averages: Mani 1.97,
  Bronson 1.48 (vs ~4 on wins). On ~71% of lost calls the close simply never happened.
  When you see a call drift to a soft ending, that IS the biggest mistake — say so.
- FRAME IS THE SECOND SEPARATOR (won 4.4 vs lost 2.9). Frame losses look like: unprompted
  justifying, apologizing for the price, chasing after a stall, answering credibility
  challenges defensively.
- PERSONAL-LEVEL PAIN predicts wins (reached on 40-52% of wins vs 11-35% of losses).
  Surface pain ("we need more leads") is not discovery. The winning probe reaches what
  it costs the OWNER personally.
- MANI'S SIGNATURE WINNING MOVES (use as the standard, quote them as model language):
  - Assumptive payment-mechanics close: "What card do you want to use?" (~90% of his
    win-close attempts). Never leaves without a decision or a dated next step.
  - Discount discipline: retainer never cut; concessions traded for a named give
    (testimonial, referral, start date).
  - Kind-not-nice pattern-naming: naming the prospect's stall pattern directly and
    forcing a real yes/no instead of a polite maybe.
  - Permission reframe: "regardless of whether we work together..." to lower defenses
    while keeping frame.
  - Abundance posture: "I love your money, but I don't need it."
- BENCHMARKS THE CLOSERS SHOULD ALSO BE HELD TO (things the closers do WELL — protect
  them): locking a specific dated next step even without payment; collecting live
  payment on the call; the family-level pain probe.

OFFER CANON — 12-WEEK / 3-CYCLE TERMS (COMPLIANCE-CRITICAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Source: Core Offer Change — Tutorial for Closers (2026-07) + Mani's directive 2026-07-11.
On any call that reaches pricing/close territory, the closer MUST verbally cover ALL of:

C1. BILLING VS TERM: billing runs in 4-week cycles, but the contract term is 12 weeks
    = 3 cycles minimum. Framed positively ("we don't do long-term contracts — 12 weeks,
    that's it").
C2. CYCLE-1 EXPECTATION SET: cycle 1 is explicitly called out as the SLOWEST cycle —
    the pixel needs time to season, results ramp. The client must hear, in plain words,
    that they should NOT expect the full promised results in cycle 1.
C3. GUARANTEE MECHANICS: if we miss the appointment target (within the 20% margin of
    error), the client only pays for the appointments we actually delivered — the
    shortfall is deducted against the retainer (per-appointment rate = retainer /
    guaranteed number). "You will never overpay us."
C4. CYCLES 2-3 PROMISE: cycles 2 and 3 are where we hit the appointment guarantees,
    no matter what — stated explicitly, not implied.
C5. CATASTROPHIC-MISS ESCAPE: if cycle 1 lands under half of the promised number
    (e.g., fewer than 7 of 15), the client can break the contract and walk.

Scoring: each element missing or mumbled on a closing call = named in `missed` with
timestamp and deducted. A close that skips C1+C2 entirely is a serious compliance
failure regardless of outcome — this is exactly what creates refunds and churn.
If the call never reached pricing/close territory, mark these "N/A — call ended
pre-close" rather than penalizing.

ONBOARDING-WALKTHROUGH COMPLIANCE (COMPLIANCE-CRITICAL)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

On any call that reaches close/payment territory, the closer MUST walk the onboarding
material with the prospect:

O1. CREATIVES SHOWN: actually shows/walks example creatives — what the ads look like.
O2. CREATIVE COLLECTION EXPLAINED: thoroughly explains what WE need from the CLIENT —
    the specific pictures/videos they must provide, and when.
O3. ASSETS FOLDER SHOWN: shows or explicitly references the assets folder(s) the client
    will use to deliver those materials.

Scoring: same treatment as the offer canon — missing elements are named in `missed`
with timestamps. "N/A — call ended pre-close" when the call legitimately never got there.

CALL-TYPE FAIRNESS RULES (fixes known scoring glitches)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INCOMPLETE CALLS ARE NOT SCORED. Classify as "incomplete" when the recording cut off
mid-conversation, the prospect dropped and never returned, tech failure ended it, or
there is too little substantive content to judge fairly (e.g., under ~10 minutes with
no discovery or pitch content). An incomplete call gets NO scorecard, NO scores, NO
compliance rating — only a short not-scored note. IMPORTANT distinctions:
- A SHORT BUT COMPLETE call (e.g., a legitimate 20-minute disqualification that reached
  a natural end) IS scoreable — score it on what it was.
- A FOLLOW-UP call is NOT incomplete — classify it as qualifying with is_followup=true.

FOLLOW-UP CALLS GET A FAIR RUBRIC. Score only the categories the call actually exercised;
set categories that already happened on a prior call (typically discovery, sometimes
authority) to null rather than penalizing. Judge the FULL engagement arc. A tight
follow-up that advances or closes the deal deserves a high score even if this specific
call contained no discovery.
